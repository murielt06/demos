The method

I don’t arrive with rules. I arrive at your apartment and we empty the closet onto the bed. Everything comes out, including the things you have not touched in four years and keep for reasons you have never said out loud.

Then we put back only what you would wear this month. It is usually a third of what was there. The rest gets sorted into sell, tailor, and let go, and I give you the name of the person who does each one.

Nothing gets bought in the first session. You cannot choose well from inside a mess, and most people own more than they think.
