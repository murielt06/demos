Transformations

Twelve people, and what actually changed.

Six of them are here. The other six preferred not to be, which I think tells you something about how this works.

Before
After

Claire, 41 · Back to an office after seven years

Seven years at home with two children, then a job in midtown with a dress code nobody wrote down. She had bought four blazers in a panic and worn none of them.

We kept two of the four. The problem was never the blazers, it was that she had nothing to put under them that was not a weekend shirt. Six pieces closed it.

Before
After

Sophie, 36 · Two sizes up, same closet

Her body had changed over eighteen months and her closet had not. She was wearing the same three things and had started avoiding photographs.

We put everything that no longer fit into storage rather than donation, because deciding that is a separate day. Then we dressed the body in the room. She bought nine things over four months.

Before
After

Nadia, 52 · Promoted, and nothing fit the room

She ran a team of four and now runs forty. Her clothes still said she was in the room to do the work, not to decide.

Very little was wrong. We changed the proportions, moved her to better fabric in the same colours, and had five things altered. She says people interrupt her less.

Before
After

Helen, 29 · Three years working from home

She had not bought anything that was not elastic since 2023 and had an interview in nine days.

One consultation, one shopping day. We bought four things. She got the job, which is not my doing, but she was not thinking about her sleeves in the room.

Before
After

Marcus, 44 · Divorced, and dressed by someone else for twelve years

Everything in the closet had been chosen by his ex-wife. He had good taste and no practice using it.

The edit was brutal and he was fine with it. What surprised him was how much he kept. He had been reading the clothes as hers rather than as clothes.

Before
After

Priya, 38 · A founder who kept being mistaken for the assistant

Pitching investors in a sector where she was usually the only woman in the room and often the youngest person in it.

We did not make her look older. We made her look like the person who decides, which is a different thing and mostly about fit, shoe and outerwear.

Nobody has told me I look different. Four people have asked if I had been away.

Nadia K., 52
